FIELD GUIDE

One match. Three ways to win.

Four players join the same room from separate devices. Everyone readies up, then selects a loadout. All points carry into the final leaderboard.

Move & aim
WASD or arrow keys. Aim with your mouse.
Attack
Hold left mouse. With Bow, hold to charge and release to fire. Release in the gold 85–95% window for Perfect Draw.
Abilities
Q: weapon special. E: element ability. Space: Sword & Shield stance. Hold Shift to guard in defensive stance.
Revive
In the raid, stand near a downed ally and hold R for 3 seconds. Taking damage interrupts. Your revive cooldown is 30 seconds.
Scoring
FFA: +1 elimination; +2 extra for elemental finishing damage; +3 stage win. 2v2: +3 each for stage victory; +2 for eliminating both opponents in a round. Raid: damage, revives and time alive each award placement points; victory gives everyone +3.
Sudden death & ties
FFA tied leaders fight without respawns in a shrinking arena. 2v2 timeout uses remaining HP, then overtime, then round damage. Raid ties use shared ranks (4/4/2/1); an all-zero category awards 1 each.
Retry
One retry after a team wipe before the raid timer expires. Change at least one loadout choice; an unchanged loadout receives the next element automatically.

Prototype balance: 100 player HP · 4,200 boss HP · single arena. Sound is synthesized locally. Mobile controls are experimental; keyboard and mouse recommended.
